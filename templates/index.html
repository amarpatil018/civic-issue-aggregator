ABOUT CLEAN CITY

Building cleaner and smarter communities.

Clean City is a smart civic issue reporting platform that connects citizens with municipal authorities.

Citizens can report problems such as potholes, garbage, broken streetlights, water leakage and waterlogging. Each complaint can include a photo and GPS location so authorities can identify and resolve problems more efficiently.
